Friends

If you’re not on here, don’t worry, you’re still my loveliest friend. This is not an official friend registry.

This is an appreciation page for my friends’ online projects.

  • Dylan’s cagedotlog.com - Dylan’s always doing some unhinged weirdo, might I even say wacko, shit. I love the complete unnavigable mess of beautiful things that this site is. In my opinion, this is how every site should be.
  • Quinten’s Indigo Chamber - Quinten is so freakishly cool and their site is incredible. I love being in space (Rotterdam) and seeing all the awesome things that happen there.
  • Ma3ke’s dwangschematiek.nl - Marieke’s a great character in my life who occasionally blesses me with her presence, despite our chaos. Her loving fondness of the handcrafted and ‘gezellig’ really shines through in every bit of this wonderful place.
  • Sytse’s neocity - AAAH this is such a treat! I didn’t even know you had a site Sytse! I love the look of this place it’s so cozy.
  • Ties’ games - A lovely, minimalist site for a principle game designer. PLEASE BRING BACK THE PLANE GAME.
  • Robin’s ulftenaar.nl - A site as unapologetically comfortable, grounded and true-to-the-self as the person. I’m instantly calmed.
  • Inne’s site - My dad runs a lovely portfolio full of fun printing and photography related works. I’m incredibly proud of him for having developed himself this much in the realm of artistry and I’m glad he’s having this much fun.
  • Robin Feather’s site - Banging tunes and fire background shit, also banging tunes!
  • Damian & Zoë - I BEG OF YOU MAKE A SITE I KNOW IT’LL BE COOL I KNOW YOU BOUGHT A DOMAIN IN LIKE 2020 CMON.
  • Mikkel - RIP mailapipebomb.com, forever in our mailboxes.
